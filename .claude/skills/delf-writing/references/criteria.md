# Writing criteria and mark estimate

The criterion names are the official ones for DELF B1 written production. The level descriptions and the point
weights below are **our own**, written to give a consistent estimate. They are not the examiners' grid, so read
the /25 as an indication with a margin of about two points.

Rate each criterion **below**, **B1** or **B1+**, then choose a value inside the band.

| Criterion (id) | below | B1 | B1+ | Max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Réalisation de la tâche (`tache`) | 0–3 | 3.5–5.5 | 6–7 | 7 |
| Cohérence et cohésion (`coherence`) | 0–1.5 | 2–3 | 3.5–4 | 4 |
| Adéquation sociolinguistique (`socioling`) | 0–0.5 | 1–1.5 | 2 | 2 |
| Lexique (`lexique`) | 0–2.5 | 3–4.5 | 5–6 | 6 |
| Morphosyntaxe (`morphosyntaxe`) | 0–2.5 | 3–4.5 | 5–6 | 6 |

## What each level looks like

**Tâche**
- below: off topic, a required item missing, opinion without reasons, or clearly under 160 words.
- B1: genre respected, every item covered, experience told, opinion backed by at least one reason and one example.
- B1+: ideas developed with detail and nuance, examples precise, the text does what a real reader would need.

**Cohérence et cohésion**
- below: one block or a list of sentences; the reader has to work out the order.
- B1: paragraphs, a clear opening and closing, common connectors used correctly, pronouns refer clearly.
- B1+: varied connectors, smooth transitions, punctuation supports the meaning.

**Adéquation sociolinguistique**
- below: wrong or mixed tu/vous, no greeting or sign-off, tone unsuited to the reader.
- B1: register consistent, standard opening and closing for the genre.
- B1+: tone adjusted finely (polite requests, softened criticism).

**Lexique**
- below: words missing or borrowed from another language, many misspellings, meaning sometimes lost.
- B1: enough words for the topic, some repetition and rewording, spelling of common words right.
- B1+: precise and varied words, some collocations and idiomatic expressions, rare misspellings.

**Morphosyntaxe**
- below: basic agreements and conjugations often wrong, tenses confused, sentences hard to follow.
- B1: simple sentences and frequent tenses mostly right; errors appear in complex sentences without blocking meaning.
- B1+: complex sentences (relatives, hypothesis, subjunctive) attempted and mostly right; few agreement errors.

## Rules of thumb

- Count each error type once when rating: ten missing accents are one weakness, not ten.
- A text that is short but otherwise good: Tâche drops to the bottom of B1 or below; the other criteria are rated on what is there.
- Do not let one criterion leak into another: a grammar-heavy text can still fully answer the task.
- Round the total to the nearest half point.
