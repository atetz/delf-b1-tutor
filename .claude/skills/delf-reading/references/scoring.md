# Reading: marking

Our own scheme, built to total 25 like the exam paper. An estimate, not an official conversion.

## Full paper (three exercises, 45 min)

| Exercise | Points | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Matching | 8 | 0.5 per correct cell of the grid when it has 16 cells (4 requirements × 4 options); scale other grid sizes to 8. No separate point for the final choice. |
| 2. Article | 8 | see item values below, scaled to 8 |
| 3. Article | 9 | see item values below, scaled to 9 |

## Item values inside an article

| Item | Points | Marking |
|---|---|---|
| Multiple choice | 1 | right or wrong |
| Vrai / faux + justification | 1.5 | 0.5 for V/F, 1 for a quotation that proves it. Right V/F with a wrong or missing quotation: 0.5. Wrong V/F: 0, whatever the quotation. |
| Who thinks what | 1 | right or wrong |
| Meaning in context | 1 | right or wrong |
| Short open answer | 2 | 2 complete, 1 partly right or too vague, 0 wrong. Language errors are not penalised if the meaning is clear. |

Add the article's raw points, divide by its raw maximum, multiply by the exercise's points, round to the nearest half.

## Part sessions

- Two exercises done under exam timing: scale to 25 (points earned ÷ points possible × 25) and record as Latest.
- One exercise, or guided mode: give the percentage, record it in the log row only.
- Over time by more than 10%: mark as usual, then note the overrun next to the score.

## After marking

Sort what was lost:
- V/F right but proof missing, wrong or paraphrased; open answers too short → `comprehension/ce-justification`;
- wrong passage, grid errors, missed condition or arithmetic → `comprehension/ce-reperage`;
- a wrong answer caused by one unknown or false-friend word → `learner/vocabulary.md` or `lexique/faux-amis`.

Record one weak-point row per pattern, not one per wrong answer.
