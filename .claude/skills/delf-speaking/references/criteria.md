# Speaking criteria and mark estimate

The criterion names are the official ones for DELF B1 oral production. The level descriptions and the point
weights below are **our own**, written to give a consistent estimate. They are not the examiners' grid, so read
the /25 as an indication with a margin of about two points.

Rate each line **below**, **B1** or **B1+**, then choose a value inside the band.

| Criterion (id) | below | B1 | B1+ | Max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tâche 1: entretien (`tache/po-entretien`) | 0–1 | 1.5–2 | 2.5–3 | 3 |
| Tâche 2: interaction (`tache/po-interaction`) | 0–2 | 2.5–4 | 4.5–5 | 5 |
| Tâche 3: point de vue (`tache/po-point-de-vue`) | 0–2.5 | 3–4.5 | 5–6 | 6 |
| Lexique (`lexique`), all three parts | 0–1.5 | 2–3 | 3.5–4 | 4 |
| Morphosyntaxe (`morphosyntaxe`), all three parts | 0–2 | 2.5–4 | 4.5–5 | 5 |
| Système phonologique (`phonologie`) | 0–0.5 | 1–1.5 | 2 | 2 |

**Phonologie cannot be heard here.** Answers arrive as typed or dictated text. Leave it unrated (`-`), add the
other lines (max 23) and scale: estimate = sum × 25 / 23. Dictation gives one weak signal only: words the
speech-to-text keeps mishearing may point to a pronunciation issue worth mentioning, never worth scoring.

Only some parts run: rate those lines, report « part estimate » and do not compute a /25.

## What each level looks like

**Tâche 1, entretien**
- below: one-word or one-sentence answers, the examiner has to drag out information.
- B1: introduces themself without help, answers about present, past and plans with a detail or reason each time.
- B1+: develops, reacts naturally to follow-ups, tenses move easily between time frames.

**Tâche 2, interaction** (also carries register and politeness)
- below: does not state the problem or the request clearly, accepts anything, or the register is wrong for the relationship.
- B1: explains the situation, asks, reacts to the objection with a reason, reaches an agreement; tu/vous consistent.
- B1+: negotiates: refuses politely, proposes alternatives, checks the final agreement; tone well judged.

**Tâche 3, point de vue** (also carries structure and connectors)
- below: no clear theme or position, a few unlinked remarks, very short, or reads the document back.
- B1: names the theme, gives an opinion with at least two reasons and one example, visible beginning and end, answers the questions.
- B1+: well-built talk with nuance (concession, condition), precise examples, defends or adjusts the position under questioning.

**Lexique**
- below: frequent gaps filled with another language, meaning often unclear.
- B1: enough words for everyday topics, rewords around gaps, some wrong word choices that do not block understanding.
- B1+: precise, some idiomatic expressions, few wrong choices.

**Morphosyntaxe**
- below: present tense only, basic agreements and conjugations often wrong.
- B1: frequent structures and the main tenses mostly right in speech; slips in complex sentences; sometimes self-corrects.
- B1+: complex sentences attempted and usually right; errors rare and often self-corrected.

## Rules of thumb for spoken French

- Spoken norms are not errors: dropped « ne » in an informal role play is fine with a friend, not with a hotel
  manager. Flag it as **usage** only when the register calls for the full form.
- Self-corrections count in the learner's favour.
- Hesitation words (euh, ben, alors) are not rated.
- Round the total to the nearest half point.
