# Part 3: expression d'un point de vue (`tache/po-point-de-vue`)

10 minutes of preparation, then 5 to 7 minutes: about 3 minutes speaking alone from a short prompt, then questions.
It tests presenting a topic, taking a position and defending it with reasons and examples.

## The prompt (write a fresh one each time)

A title and 50 to 80 words in the style of a short news item or web article: a trend, a decision or a figure,
with a hint of debate. Invent the facts plausibly; no real names of people, and no real statistics presented as
true. End with the instruction:

« Vous dégagez le thème soulevé par le document et vous présentez votre opinion sous la forme d'un exposé
personnel de trois minutes environ. »

Angles that produce a debate: a new rule (phones banned in a school, a car-free centre), a changing habit
(four-day week, buying second-hand, eating less meat), a tension (tourism and residents, screens and children).

## How to run it

1. Show the prompt. « Vous avez dix minutes de préparation. Dites-moi quand vous êtes prêt. »
   In guided mode, ask for the notes (keywords only) before the monologue and react in one line.
2. Listen to the whole monologue without interrupting.
3. Ask two or three questions: one asking to clarify or expand a point, one giving a mild counter-argument
   (« Certains diraient que… Qu'en pensez-vous ? »), one linking to their own life.

## Shape of a three-minute monologue

| Step | Content | Useful openers |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Theme | What the document is about, in your own words, and the question it raises | Ce document parle de… / La question est de savoir si… |
| 2. Position | Your opinion in one sentence | Personnellement, je pense que… / À mon avis… |
| 3. Reason 1 + example | A reason, then something concrete: your experience, someone you know, your country | D'abord… / Par exemple, dans mon entreprise… |
| 4. Reason 2 + example | Another reason, or the other side and why it weighs less | Ensuite… / Il est vrai que…, mais… |
| 5. Conclusion | Sum up, add a condition or a wish | Pour conclure… / En résumé, je suis pour…, à condition que… |

## Preparation in 10 minutes

- 2 min: read, find the theme and the question. Write the question as one sentence.
- 5 min: note keywords only for position, two reasons, two examples. Never full sentences: reading aloud sounds flat and costs points.
- 3 min: write the first sentence and the last sentence in full, and four connectors down the margin.

## What a good performance does

- Starts from the document but does not read or copy it.
- Has a visible beginning, middle and end; the listener always knows where they are.
- Gives examples from real life. « Par exemple » appears at least twice.
- In the questions, answers the question asked, holds the position or adjusts it with a reason.

## Common losses

- No introduction: starting with « Je suis d'accord » before saying with what.
- Reasons without any example, or one long example and no opinion.
- No conclusion: stopping with « C'est tout ».
- Under 90 seconds of speech.
