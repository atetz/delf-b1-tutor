# Listening: marking

Our own scheme, built to total 25 like the exam paper. An estimate, not an official conversion.

## Exam mode (three documents)

| Document | Questions | Points |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6 | 6 (1 each) |
| 2 | 7 | 8 (1 each, one harder question worth 2) |
| 3 | 8 | 11 (five at 1, three at 2) |
| | 21 | 25 |

Two-point questions are the ones needing a reworded idea or a detail that was corrected in the recording.
Right or wrong only; no half points on multiple choice. Short answers: accept spelling mistakes if the word is recognisable.

## Radio mode (bulletin + transcript)

| Part | Points | How |
|---|---|---|
| 8 questions | 16 | 2 each; 1 for a half-right short answer |
| Summary | 9 | see below |

Summary, judged on understanding only, not on the French:
- **Items caught (0–4):** share of the main news items in the passage that appear in the summary.
- **Accuracy (0–3):** who, what, where are right; 1 off for each fact reversed or invented.
- **Details (0–2):** at least two correct figures, dates or causes.

Real radio is harder than the exam recordings. A radio-mode score of 15 or more suggests a comfortable pass on
this paper; say this when giving the mark.

## After marking

Sort the wrong answers:
- mostly detail questions → weak point `comprehension/co-details`;
- mostly global questions or a summary that misses the point → `comprehension/co-global`;
- a word misheard as its false friend or unknown → `lexique/faux-amis` or a row in `learner/vocabulary.md`.

Record one weak-point row per pattern, not one per wrong answer.
