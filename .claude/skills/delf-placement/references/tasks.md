# Placement tasks: structure and how to generate them

The placement is shorter than the exam but touches every task type that is marked. Same structure for the first
placement and for each checkpoint, so results can be compared. Level of all material: B1, see
`reference/b1-descriptors.md`.

## CE (20 min)

One article of 320 to 380 words on an everyday or work theme, with two people quoted who do not fully agree,
some figures, and one decision or change. Ten questions in text order:

| Count | Type | Points each |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | multiple choice, three options (one general, two detail) | 2 |
| 5 | vrai/faux with a quotation as justification (at least two true, two false) | 3 |
| 2 | short open answer | 2 |

Total 25. Write the key with the deciding passage for each question before showing the text.
More on building questions: `.claude/skills/delf-reading/references/task-types.md`.

## PE (45 min)

One instruction on the same theme as the CE text or a neighbouring one, so vocabulary is not the obstacle:
forum post or letter, asking for an experience, an opinion and reasons, 160 words minimum.
Templates: `.claude/skills/delf-writing/references/genres.md`.

## PO (15 min + 10 min preparation)

Run as the examiner, in French, following the three files in `.claude/skills/delf-speaking/references/`:
1. **Entretien** (`entretien.md`): self-introduction and four follow-up questions covering present, past, future.
2. **Interaction** (`interaction.md`): one scenario card with an objection and a second obstacle. Pick a family
   that fits the learner's life (profile).
3. **Point de vue** (`point-de-vue.md`): one prompt of 50 to 80 words, 10 minutes of preparation, monologue,
   two questions.

Before starting, ask the learner to dictate if they can, and not to tidy the transcript.

## CO (15 min)

Preferred: two recordings read by text-to-speech, each heard twice
(`.claude/skills/delf-listening/references/sources.md`, exam mode).

| Doc | Kind | Length | Questions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | a voicemail or announcement with practical details: times, a price, a change of plan | 130–160 words | 5 multiple choice, 2.5 points each |
| 2 | a short radio-style report on a local initiative, one person's story, a problem and a next step | 180–220 words | 5 multiple choice, 2.5 points each |

Give one minute to read the questions, play, pause 30 seconds, play again, take the answers.

No text-to-speech on this machine: use the radio method from the same file (listen twice to 3 minutes of a news
bulletin in easy French, summarise, paste the transcript, answer 8 questions) and mark it with the radio scheme
in `.claude/skills/delf-listening/references/scoring.md`. Note in the placement file which method was used:
radio scores run lower.

## Checkpoints

New material every time, never the sample set again. Vary the theme and the interaction family from the previous
checkpoint. Keep counts, lengths and timings identical.
