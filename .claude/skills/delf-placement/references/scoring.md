# Placement: from answers to scores and a plan

All marks are estimates on our own scale. Say so once when presenting them, and give them to the half point.

## 1. Mark each paper /25

| Paper | How |
|---|---|
| CE | Points per question as in `tasks.md`. Vrai/faux: 1 for the right V/F, 2 for a quotation that proves it; wrong V/F scores 0. Short answers: 2, 1 or 0. |
| CO | 2.5 per right answer (text-to-speech set), or the radio scheme in `.claude/skills/delf-listening/references/scoring.md`. |
| PE | Correct with `.claude/skills/delf-writing/references/correction-format.md`, rate the five criteria with `.claude/skills/delf-writing/references/criteria.md`. |
| PO | Same correction procedure on the transcript, rate with `.claude/skills/delf-speaking/references/criteria.md` (phonologie unrated, scaled). |

Total = sum of the four. Pass rule: 50/100 and at least 5 in each paper.

## 2. Weak points

Collect error types, not single errors:
- PE and PO: each group from the correction, with its topic id from `reference/topics.md`.
- CE and CO: one row per pattern of lost marks (`comprehension/…`), plus false friends or key words misunderstood.
- A task criterion rated below (no conclusion in the monologue, first compromise accepted, an item of the
  writing instruction missing) is a weak point too, with its `tache/…` id.

Rank by cost: an error type that appears in two papers, or that lowers a criterion's rating, comes first.
Keep ten at most; note the others by name in the placement file for the next checkpoint.

## 3. Phase

| Phase | When | Sessions look like |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Foundations | total under 50, or any paper under 8 | guided tasks, no timer, more grammar and vocabulary review |
| 2. Exam tasks | total 50 to 64 | full tasks under exam timing, review targets the lowest criterion |
| 3. Mocks | total 65 or more, or the last four weeks before the exam | weekly mock, sessions on what the mock exposed |

## 4. Weekly emphasis

- The lowest paper gets the second slot on Saturday.
- Two papers within one point of each other at the bottom: alternate Saturdays.
- If morphosyntaxe is rated below in both PE and PO, Friday's review is grammar for the next four weeks.

## 5. Exam window (rule of thumb, with daily one-hour sessions)

| Total now | Lowest paper | Suggested window |
|---|---|---|
| 65 or more | 10 or more | next available session; four to six weeks of format practice |
| 50 to 64 | 8 or more | in two to three months |
| 35 to 49 | any | in four to six months |
| under 35 | any | consolidate A2 first, re-test in two months before choosing a date |

A paper under 8 moves the window one row down whatever the total: the 5/25 minimum must be safe on a bad day.
Give the window as a range of months and remind the learner that exam centres set their own dates and
registration deadlines.

## 6. Checkpoints

Compare with the previous placement or checkpoint per paper and per criterion. Report what moved by more than
two points (the margin of these estimates), which weak points were retired since, and whether the phase changes.
