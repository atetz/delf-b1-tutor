# B1 grammar checklist

Our own list of the points a B1 candidate needs, roughly in order of payoff. "Topic" is the level-2 id in
`reference/topics.md` (prefix `morphosyntaxe/`). Points without a file are taught from the four-part structure
in `fle-grammar` and get a file when they become a weak point.

## Sentence basics
| Point | Topic |
|---|---|
| Élision and contracted articles (qu'il, d'apprendre, au, du) | elision |
| Agreement of determiners, adjectives and participles | accords |
| Subject-verb agreement (on, tout le monde, la plupart) | accord-verbe |
| Negation (ne…pas, plus, jamais, rien, personne; pas de) | negation |
| Question forms (est-ce que, inversion, quel, lequel) | questions (file to write) |

## Tenses and moods
| Point | Topic |
|---|---|
| Passé composé, imparfait, plus-que-parfait in a story | temps-du-recit |
| Futur simple and futur proche | futur (file to write) |
| Conditionnel présent: politeness, advice, wish | conditionnel (file to write) |
| Hypothesis with si (présent/futur, imparfait/conditionnel) | hypothese (file to write) |
| Subjonctif présent after il faut que, vouloir que, pour que, bien que | subjonctif |
| Impératif, with pronouns | imperatif (file to write) |
| Gérondif (en travaillant) | gerondif (file to write) |
| Passive voice | passif (file to write) |
| Reported speech in the present and past | discours-rapporte (file to write) |

## Pronouns and linking
| Point | Topic |
|---|---|
| Object pronouns, y, en, and their place | pronoms |
| Relative pronouns qui, que, où, dont; ce qui, ce que | relatifs |
| Verb + preposition (penser à, avoir besoin de, demander à qqn de) | prepositions |
| Comparison and superlative | comparaison (file to write) |
| Expressing cause, consequence, purpose, opposition, concession | see `coherence/connecteurs` |
| Time expressions (depuis, il y a, pendant, pour, dans, en) | expressions-de-temps (file to write) |
| Articles: partitive, and de after quantity or negation | articles (file to write) |
