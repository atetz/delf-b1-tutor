# DELF B1 tout public: exam format

Source: the public description by France Éducation International (FEI),
https://www.france-education-international.fr/diplome/delf-tout-public/niveau-b1.
Written from memory of that page on 2026-10-06: the site refused automated access that day, so check the
figures against the page (or your exam centre's convocation) before relying on a detail.

| Paper | What you do | Time | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compréhension de l'oral (CO) | Answer questionnaires on three short recordings, each played twice. About 6 minutes of audio in total. | about 25 min | /25 |
| Compréhension des écrits (CE) | Answer questionnaires on written documents: pick out the information that fits a given need, then understand general-interest texts. | 45 min | /25 |
| Production écrite (PE) | Write one text giving a personal position on a general topic (message, letter, forum post, article). In the exam two subjects are offered and you choose one. At least 160 words. | 45 min | /25 |
| Production orale (PO) | Three parts in a row with one examiner pair: guided interview, interaction, point of view from a short prompt. | about 15 min, plus 10 min preparation for part 3 | /25 |

The three written papers are sat together (1 h 55). The speaking paper is individual, on the same or another day.

## Speaking, part by part

| Part | Preparation | Length | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Entretien dirigé | none | 2 to 3 min | You introduce yourself; the examiner asks about your life, work, tastes, past and plans. |
| 2. Exercice en interaction | none | 3 to 4 min | You draw a situation and play it with the examiner: solve a problem, negotiate, complain, organise. |
| 3. Expression d'un point de vue | 10 min | 5 to 7 min | From a short prompt: state the topic, give your opinion with reasons and examples (about 3 min alone), then answer questions. |

## Passing

- Total out of 100. Pass mark: 50/100.
- Minimum 5/25 in every paper; below that in any paper, the exam is failed whatever the total.

## Question formats in the comprehension papers

The current format uses closed questions: multiple choice, vrai/faux, matching options to criteria.
Practice tasks in this repo also ask for a short justification from the text, because it forces precise reading
and shows where a wrong answer came from.

## What this means for practice

- Every paper weighs the same, so the lowest skill is where a point is cheapest to gain.
- Timing is part of the task: practice with the real time limits from phase 2 onwards.
