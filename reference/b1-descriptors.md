# What B1 means, per skill

Our own plain-language summary of the level, used to pitch tasks and to judge answers.

## Listening (CO)
- Follows the main points of clear standard speech on familiar matters: work, school, leisure, daily news.
- Gets the gist and the key facts of a short radio item or conversation when the delivery is fairly slow.
- Loses details when speech is fast, idiomatic or noisy. That is expected at B1.

## Reading (CE)
- Understands factual texts on everyday or work topics: articles, messages, notices, forum posts.
- Finds a specific piece of information in a longer document and compares options against a list of needs.
- Recognises the line of argument and the writer's opinion, without catching every nuance.

## Writing (PE)
- Writes a simple, connected text on a familiar topic: tells what happened, describes, gives an opinion with reasons.
- Organises it: opening, two or three developed ideas, closing, linked with common connectors.
- Chooses a register that suits the reader (tu or vous, greeting and sign-off).
- Vocabulary is enough to say what is needed, with some rewording around unknown words.
- Grammar: frequent structures are mostly right. Errors remain but the meaning stays clear.

## Speaking (PO)
- Talks about self, work, past experiences and plans without preparation.
- Handles an everyday situation that goes slightly wrong: explains the problem, asks, insists politely, agrees a solution.
- Gives a point of view with reasons and an example, and reacts to simple questions about it.
- Pauses and reformulations are normal; pronunciation is clear enough to be understood despite an accent.

## Below B1 and above B1, in one line each
- **Below:** isolated sentences, lists instead of reasons, the reader or listener has to guess the meaning.
- **B1+:** develops ideas with detail and nuance, self-corrects, uses less frequent structures with few errors.
